# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and experience level, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (Security → Report a vulnerability works for this too, or the email on the maintainer's profile). Reports are handled confidentially.
